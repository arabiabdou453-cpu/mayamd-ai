About MayaMD

Physicians building the AI they wanted for their own patients.

We started in 2018 with one belief: every patient deserves care that follows the evidence, every time. Today MayaMD is a nationally recognized platform that guides patients from hospital discharge through the critical months at home. We offer a comprehensive, integrated set of services, including conversational AI, a companion app, connected devices, two-way texting and clinical staff to support your clinicians and patients. Patient-specific protocols determine the questions, the AI does the talking, and your clinicians provide the right care at the right time.
