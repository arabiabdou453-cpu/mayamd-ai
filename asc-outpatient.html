How it works

Send. Generate. Engage.

Your instructions stay the source. Maya explains them; it doesn't substitute its own.

  1. 01 · Send

    Secure email

    Your staff sends the patient's prep sheet, discharge instructions or after-visit summary to Maya by secure email.

  2. 02 · Generate

    A personal conversation

    Within minutes, Maya turns it into a plain-language conversation for this patient, in their language.

  3. 03 · Engage

    A secure web link

    The patient opens it on a phone or computer, asks questions and confirms each step with teach-back.

For ambulatory surgery centers

Fewer surprises on the day. Fewer calls after.

Patients arrive prepared and go home knowing what to do, with your center's own instructions explained one step at a time.

Before

Preprocedure education and preparation

Arrival time, fasting and medication-hold instructions, transportation and what to bring, with teach-back on each step.

After

Postprocedure recovery instructions

Wound care, pain control, activity limits and the warning signs that mean call, explained once the patient is home.

GI pathways

Colonoscopy and endoscopy

Built from your center's prep instructions: diet changes, the bowel-prep timeline, medication holds, day-of logistics and what to expect afterward.

For physician practices

The visit ends. The explanation doesn't.

For the moments patients leave with the most to remember: a new diagnosis, a new medicine, and the questions that come up at home.

New diagnosis

New diagnosis education

What the diagnosis means, what happens next and what to watch for, using your practice's own education and plan.

New medicine

New medication initiation

Why it was prescribed, how and when to take it, which side effects to expect and which ones need a call, confirmed with teach-back.

At home

Follow-up questions and escalation

Patients ask follow-up questions in their own language. Answers that point to a problem, and questions your instructions don't cover, go to your team with the conversation attached.

Questions centers and practices ask

Which procedures can you support?

Maya works from the instructions you already give patients, so each pathway is built from your own documents. We'll pick your first procedures together on the demo call.

Do patients need an app?

No. Patients open their conversation from a secure web link on a phone or computer.

Does Maya give medical advice?

Maya explains your instructions and checks understanding. Questions that need clinical judgment go to your team.

Which languages are supported?

The platform supports 32 languages, and patients choose theirs.

Start with one procedure.

Pick one procedure or one new-diagnosis pathway and send the first instructions by secure email. A 20-minute demo is the first step.
