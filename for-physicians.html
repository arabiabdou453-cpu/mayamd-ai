Questions physicians ask

Is this a chatbot?

No. Validated clinical logic decides every question; the AI only phrases them and summarizes answers.

Does it work with our EHR?

Yes. Notes and documentation go into your existing workflow. Start with EHR access; add a feed later.

Who bills?

MayaMD bills TCM by default. Your clinicians bill RPM, APCM, PCM and BHI under your own NPIs, so those programs add revenue. If your clinicians bill TCM too, our fee comes from that new revenue. Our staff handles enrollment, documentation and claims prep.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Encrypted, HIPAA compliant and covered by a signed BAA.
