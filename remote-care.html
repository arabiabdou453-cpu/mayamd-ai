Symptom triage, built in

A worrying symptom at night doesn’t have to mean the ER.

Between monthly check-ins, patients can report a new or worsening symptom by app or text at any time. Maya’s symptom triage asks the follow-up questions a clinician would, recommends the right level of care and writes a note for your team. True emergencies go to the ER right away; lower-risk concerns are routed to your clinicians or a telemedicine visit instead.

Questions practices ask

Which programs fit my practice?

Primary care practices usually lead with APCM and add RPM and BHI where they fit. Specialty practices often use PCM for one complex condition, plus RPM. We check eligibility and billing rules for each patient.

Do patients need devices?

Not for APCM, PCM or BHI. Devices are part of RPM, and we provide and support them.

What does my team do?

Review care plans and act on escalations. Our staff does the enrollment, outreach, documentation and claims prep.

Who qualifies?

Most Medicare patients who receive ongoing care from your practice. We screen your panel for you.

Is it compliant?

HIPAA compliant with a signed BAA, and workflows built around CMS program requirements.
