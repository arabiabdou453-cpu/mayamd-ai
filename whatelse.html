#WhatElse · For patients and families

What else could this be?

The most important question in medicine is often the one nobody asks. Maya helps you understand your symptoms and walk into your next appointment with better questions.

Free. Takes about five minutes.
Why it matters

Most of us will face a diagnostic error at least once.

That's the conclusion of the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine's 2015 report on diagnosis. Doctors work under time pressure, symptoms overlap, and the first answer isn't always the right one. Asking "what else could this be?" helps you and your doctor consider the possibilities together.

QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

"What else could this be?"

"What would make you think it's something else?"

"When should I come back if it doesn't get better?"
